# 📱 Auto-Posting & Account Safety Guide

This guide explains how to set up automated and semi-automated posting for your clipped videos across **X (Twitter), YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok** while avoiding shadowbans, action blocks, and account suspensions.

---

## ⚠️ Why Accounts Get Banned / Shadowbanned

| Platform | Major Trigger | Consequence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Instagram** | Using unofficial bot scripts, headless browsers, or third-party password logins. | "Action Blocked (7 Days)" or Shadowban (0 views on Reels). |
| **TikTok** | Posting via data center IPs on brand new unverified accounts without mobile telemetry. | "0-View Jail" (Algorithm stops showing videos to anyone). |
| **YouTube** | Uploading 3+ videos daily via unverified API scripts on a fresh channel without human history. | Channel terminated for "Spam, scams, or commercially deceptive practices". |
| **X (Twitter)** | Posting at identical clockwork intervals (e.g. exactly every 120 mins) without user interaction. | Account labeled as automated bot, reduced timeline reach. |

---

## 🛡️ The 4 Golden Rules for 100% Account Safety

### 1. The 7-Day Account Warm-Up Period (CRITICAL)
Never connect automation or auto-publishers to a brand-new account on Day 1.
* **Days 1–3**: Log in from your real mobile phone. Watch content, search gaming tags, like and comment on 3–5 posts daily.
* **Days 4–7**: Manually upload your first 3 clips directly from the mobile app. Add trending sounds from inside the app.
* **Result**: The platform registers your device ID and IP address as a legitimate, authentic human creator.

### 2. Never Use Password-Based Scraper Bots
* Only use official **OAuth integrations** (Meta Business Partners, YouTube Data API OAuth, or verified multi-platform dispatchers like Buffer, Metricool, or Upload-Post).
* If a tool asks for your raw username and password, **do not use it** — Meta and TikTok flag third-party server logins instantly.

### 3. Add Randomized Delay (Jitter)
* Do not publish on exact clockwork intervals (e.g., exactly 12:00:00, 14:00:00).
* Space out posts between **2 hours 15 minutes** and **2 hours 45 minutes** with random variance. Natural human posting is never synchronized to the second.

### 4. The Safest Agency Method: Semi-Auto (Draft Mode)
Top social media clipping agencies use **Semi-Automation**:
1. OpenShorts cuts, frames (16:9 for X, 9:16 for Shorts/Reels), and captions all 3 daily clips automatically.
2. The clips are pushed to Google Drive, Telegram channel, or scheduled via a reminder tool.
3. You tap **"Publish"** from your phone in 5 seconds.
* **Benefit**: 100% of editing effort is automated, but the upload signal originates from your genuine phone, guaranteeing **0% ban risk and maximum organic reach**.

## 🛠️ Recommended Safe Auto-Posting Setups

### Option A: Free Official Schedulers (Recommended for Beginners)
1. **Buffer / Metricool / Later**:
   * Official partners with Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and X.
   * Free tiers allow scheduling up to 30 posts per month.
   * Connect your accounts via official OAuth (no passwords stored).
   * Set your 3 daily slots: Morning (13:00 IST), Evening (17:30 IST), Night (21:30 IST).

### Option B: Developer API Dispatcher (Upload-Post API)
* Uses official webhooks and OAuth tokens.
* Requires registering an app at [app.upload-post.com](https://app.upload-post.com/api-keys).
* Ensure your accounts are warmed up for at least 7–10 days before sending batch API calls.
